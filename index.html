How we work

From measure to walkthrough.

  1. Measure

    We visit, measure every room and check the subfloor for level and moisture.

  2. Choose

    See samples in your own light and get a written quote with materials and labor.

  3. Prep

    Old flooring comes out, the subfloor is leveled, and baseboards are protected or removed.

  4. Install

    Professional installation with clean transitions, trim and thresholds.

  5. Walkthrough

    We walk the finished floor with you and leave care instructions for your material.
